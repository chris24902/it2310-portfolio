Welcome to Lab 2

In Lab 2, I am learning how to build a multi‑page website and connect those pages using both relative and absolute links. This lab also introduces important web development skills such as creating accessible HTML tables and organizing content across different pages within a portfolio repository. In addition to practicing essential HTML navigation techniques, I’m also improving my workflow by making meaningful Git commits with descriptive messages. Overall, this lab helps strengthen the foundation needed for creating well‑structured, user‑friendly websites.
